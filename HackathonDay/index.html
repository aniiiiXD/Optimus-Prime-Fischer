04 / Next gates

Questions that change the agent

A candidate must beat OneVOne on fresh board seeds, from both seats, with legal moves, no game caps, and a paired win-rate improvement before promotion.

  1. 01What are the released competition rules, observation contract, runtime cap and opponent set?
  2. 02Which action class causes the largest paired win loss: opening, road access, trade conversion, robber, development purchase or defense?
  3. 03Can a win-trained state/action value model improve OneVOne without disturbing its tactical choices?
  4. 04Can search sample hidden states from public evidence and beat the incumbent on fresh boards?
