2026-10-17Measured baseline

Learning-compatible OneVOne architecture and trained neural scorers

Question
Make strategic preferences learnable while preserving legal actions and bounded guaranteed-win protection.
Setup
Separate experiment/onevone-learnable worktree. Versioned visible board/action features, compressed expert ranks, replaceable MLP/transformer scorer, configurable blend, separate win guard. Final training: 512 games plus 423 continuations, 10,354 examples; whole-board train/validation split.
Result
Both networks trained and exported to NumPy. Nine behavior checks pass. The chosen MLP changed 42/368 actual audited decisions after guards, with zero model failures. MLP and transformer passed official-clock smoke tests.
Learning
A neural network can guide this heuristic without changing the rules engine. Active model influence is demonstrated; prediction loss and changed moves do not establish improved winning strength.
Next
Use the preserved official baseline and the same-expert control when measuring subsequent learning methods.

2026-10-17Measured baseline

Guaranteed-win and Monopoly decision audit

Question
Find concrete decision mistakes without changing the playing agent.
Setup
1,199 saved masked positions; bounded deterministic search at 294 selected late-game positions, depth up to three actions; exact-resource scoring of 33 selected Monopoly plays.
Result
Found 13 positions across 11 games with guaranteed winning alternatives: ten development purchases and three end-turn decisions. Five Monopoly plays took zero cards; nine chose a resource with a lower exact successor score. All of these findings were in games eventually won.
Learning
The development-card shortcut, two-action search depth and production-based Monopoly estimate have reproducible limitations. These findings do not explain the 73 losses and do not establish a win-rate gain from fixing them.
Next
Test exact visible resource transfers and guaranteed-win handling separately, then investigate the broader early-game deficit.
